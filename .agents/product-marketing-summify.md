# Product Marketing Context — Summify

*Last updated: 2026-10-03*  
*Status: Draft from 12-month vision + future-education thesis alignment*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** AI Understanding Engine that turns any information into understanding.  
**What it does:** Upload PDF/PPTX/YouTube/articles → knowledge representation → teach, test, remember, apply (not just summarize).  
**Product category:** AI study / understanding workspace → long-term Personal Learning OS  
**Product type:** Consumer SaaS (+ future Team/API)  
**Business model:** Free / Scholar / Pro / Team; SEO free utilities → conversion

## Target Audience
**Primary:** University & exam students; self-taught professionals drowning in papers/videos  
**Jobs to be done:**
- Turn source material into lasting understanding
- Know what I don’t know; fix misconceptions
- Prepare for exams from *my* notes/lectures  
**Anti-persona (near-term):** K-12 school procurement as primary wedge; “replace schools” buyers

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Information overload; ChatGPT/NotebookLM help chat or summarize but don’t maintain durable mastery of *your* sources.  
**Alternatives:** ChatGPT Study, NotebookLM, Quizlet, Khanmigo, generic summarizers  
**Why they fall short:** No durable dual graph (source + learner), weak misconception loop, curriculum lock-in or source-chat only

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Understanding representation (concepts, claims, relations, evidence)
- User knowledge state + adaptive teach/test/reteach
- Any-material path (not only owned curriculum)
- Cross-source synthesis  
**Promise:** Don’t just summarize it. Understand it.  
**Avoid claiming:** We abolish schools/teachers in 10 years (use as internal thesis only)

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** AI summarizers, NotebookLM  
**Secondary:** Chat tutors (ChatGPT Study), curriculum tutors (Knowunity, Khanmigo)  
**Indirect:** Human tutors, Anki-only workflows

## Goals
**Business:** Fundable AI learning infrastructure; global (EN) + Scholar pricing  
**Product north star:** Measured understanding gains on user-uploaded sources  
**Near-term focus:** AI Tutor + Knowledge Graph + Exam Simulator + Adaptive memory
